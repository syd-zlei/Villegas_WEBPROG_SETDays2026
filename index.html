OUR MISSION

Why SET Week Exists

NU Fairview SET Week 2026 is a proposed three-day event for the School of Engineering and Technology community. It is designed to bring students together through activities that celebrate learning, creativity, and the practical use of technology.

From programming and web development to robotics and user experience design, the proposed activities encourage participants to explore different areas of technology. Students can share ideas, practice problem-solving, and learn from one another in a collaborative environment.

The event is intended for NU Fairview students, faculty members, and invited participants interested in engineering and technology. Through friendly competitions, demonstrations, and learning sessions, SET Week aims to encourage curiosity, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

Event Dates: December 2–4, 2026
Organizer: NU Fairview School of Engineering and Technology
